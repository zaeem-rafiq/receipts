# Bramblefin: Seed Deck (September 2026)

## Slide 1: Bramblefin
Your Stripe, your books and your ARR, reconciled every night.
Seed round. New York, NY. Confidential; synthetic demo deck for a fictional company with fictional customers.

## Slide 2: The problem
Every subscription software company reports ARR, and almost none of them can tie it to the books. Stripe says one number, QuickBooks says another, the board deck says a third. Finance teams rebuild it by hand every month and get it wrong at the worst moment: fundraising and audit.

## Slide 3: The product
Bramblefin is billing-to-books reconciliation and ARR reporting for subscription software companies. Connect Stripe, the general ledger and the CRM; every charge is classified as recurring or one-time, every contract is recognized ratably, and ARR, NRR and deferred revenue reconcile to the dollar every night with the rows behind them.

SaaS subscription: Starter from $500/mo, Growth $1,000 to $2,000/mo, Scale $2,000 to $6,000/mo, Enterprise from $6,000/mo. Implementation packages from $2,500. Annual prepay discount 10%.

## Slide 7: Market
Over 100,000 subscription software companies worldwide bill through Stripe or a similar platform. At $12k ACV that is a $1.2B serviceable market.

## Slide 8: Competition
Revenue-recognition suites serve the Fortune 2000 and take six months to implement. Spreadsheets serve everyone else. Bramblefin is the first billing-to-books platform priced and shaped for seed to Series C software companies.
